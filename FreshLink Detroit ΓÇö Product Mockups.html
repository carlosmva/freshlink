Product Concept

One platform, three sides.
AI-coordinated food access.

FreshLink connects community facilities with local food suppliers and delivery partners. These nine screens show the full workflow: facilities plan and approve AI-recommended bulk orders, suppliers list inventory and accept recurring institutional demand, and transportation partners run consolidated routes. All data shown is illustrative pilot-scenario data.

🏠

Client — Facility staff

Shelter, group home, or senior facility buyer. Plans weekly meals, reviews the AI-recommended basket, approves substitutions, tracks delivery and impact. Mobile app.

🌾

Partner — Food source

Food hub, farm, grocer, or surplus partner. Publishes inventory and pricing, receives recurring consolidated orders, forecasts demand. Web portal.

🚐

Partner — Transportation

Logistics or EV-fleet operator. Receives consolidated multi-stop routes with temperature requirements, confirms proof of delivery, tracks earnings. Web portal.
