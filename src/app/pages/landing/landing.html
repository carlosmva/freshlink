Detroit food-access network

One platform, three sides. AI-coordinated food access.

FreshLink is a Detroit food-access network: community facilities, local suppliers, and delivery partners share one loop so meals are planned, sourced, and delivered without wasteful one-off runs.

Sign in to a portal

Client — Facility staff

Plan weekly meals, review the AI basket, approve substitutions, track impact.

Partner — Food source

Publish inventory and pricing, receive recurring orders, forecast demand.

Partner — Transportation

Run consolidated EV routes, confirm proof of delivery, track earnings.
Mission

Get the right food to community tables — on time, in budget, from nearby.

Shelters, kitchens, and neighborhood programs should not have to juggle wholesalers, last-minute substitutions, and separate delivery apps. FreshLink coordinates weekly demand, local inventory, and last-mile routes in one place. AI recommends bulk baskets that fit menus and budgets, surfaces Michigan-grown and surplus items, and keeps substitutions explicit so staff stay in control.

Vision

A regional network where buying is predictable and surplus is rescued.

Growers and food hubs see recurring institutional volume weeks ahead. Near-best-before stock moves through a surplus lane instead of the dumpster. Transportation partners run dense EV routes — one pickup, several nearby stops — not scattered errands. Facilities can show funders meals supported, dollars saved, and local spend in grant-ready form.
